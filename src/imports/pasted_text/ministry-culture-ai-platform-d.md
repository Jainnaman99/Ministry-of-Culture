Design a complete, production-grade AI-powered semantic search and conversational chatbot platform for the Ministry of Culture (Government of India). The interface should feel authoritative, premium, and modern, combining government trust with cutting-edge AI UX.

🎨 DESIGN STYLE:

* Theme: Premium Government + AI (blend of NIC, MeitY, and modern SaaS)
* Colors: Deep navy blue (#0B1F3B), ivory white (#F8F6F2), subtle gold accents (#C6A75E)
* Typography: Serif headings (Playfair Display), clean sans-serif body (Inter)
* Layout: Clean, minimal, high whitespace, grid-based
* UI feel: Calm, intelligent, trustworthy, not flashy
